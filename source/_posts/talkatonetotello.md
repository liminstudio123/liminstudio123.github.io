### **Talkatone 转出号码教程**

#### **中文版：**

**从 Talkatone 转出您的号码是一个简单的过程。虽然 Talkatone 不使用传统的帐号，但您仍可以按照以下步骤将您的号码转至其他运营商：**

1. **请求您的端口 PIN**
   首先，您需要从 Talkatone 请求您的端口 PIN。请仔细阅读更新后的端口政策。
2. **发送移植请求电子邮件**
   发送电子邮件至 [Support@Talkatone.com](mailto:Support@Talkatone.com)，主题行中注明“移植请求”。在电子邮件正文中，请包含：
   * 您希望移植的 Talkatone 号码。
   * 帐户上的姓名。
   * 您的帐户的创建日期或将电话号码分配给您的帐户的日期。
   * **您要移植到的新运营商的名称。**
3. **移植信息**
   您的新运营商应该能够仅使用您的 Talkatone 号码和您收到的 PIN 来完成移植。
4. **帐户关闭**
   一旦端口完成，您的 Talkatone 帐户将被永久关闭。这意味着：
   * 您将无法访问您的帐户和任何相关数据。
   * 您将无法重新激活该帐户。
   * 您不能重复使用与 Talkatone 帐户关联的电子邮件、电话号码或任何关联的 Apple、Facebook 或 Google 帐户。

**通过遵循以下步骤，您可以轻松地将您的 Talkatone 号码移植到其他服务。** 请确保做好相应的计划，因为移植完成后，您的 Talkatone 帐户将永久关闭。

#### **English Version:**

**Porting out your number from Talkatone is a straightforward process. While Talkatone does not use traditional account numbers, you can still port your number to another carrier by following these steps:**

1. **Request Your Port PIN**
   To begin, you’ll need to request your port PIN from Talkatone. Review the updated port policy carefully.
2. **Send a Port Request Email**
   Email [Support@Talkatone.com](mailto:Support@Talkatone.com) with "Port Request" in the subject line. In the body of the email, include:
   * The Talkatone number you wish to port out.
   * The name on the account.
   * The date your account was created or when the phone number was assigned to your account.
   * **The name of the new carrier you are porting to.**
3. **Porting Information**
   Your new carrier should be able to complete the port with just your Talkatone number and the PIN you receive.
4. **Account Closure**
   Once the port is completed, your Talkatone account will be permanently closed. This means:
   * You will lose access to your account and any associated data.
   * You will not be able to reactivate the account.
   * You cannot reuse the email, phone number, or any linked Apple, Facebook, or Google accounts associated with the Talkatone account.
